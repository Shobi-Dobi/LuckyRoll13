Founder and coach

Shabi Shilon — Boxing and Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu

I opened Lucky Roll13 for the community.

My name is Shabi Shilon. I founded Lucky Roll13 and coach Boxing and Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (BJJ). I teach from ongoing experience in coaching, training, and competition in Israel and abroad.

In September 2026, I represented Lucky Roll13 at the ADCC Amateur World Championship in Kraków, Poland, competing in the Men's Master Professional division. The official event listing and match are available on Smoothcomp and on FloGrappling.

My goal is to reach as many hearts as possible. To be a teacher, bring people together, and create a place where we are there for one another.

When I opened Lucky Roll13, I did not want to create only a place where people learn to fight. I wanted to create a school that develops the teachers of the future. The children growing up in my school today will be tomorrow’s teachers, coaches, and community members who lead others and do good around them.

Lucky Roll13 is a school for Boxing, Jiu-Jitsu and Mixed Martial Arts — MMA. a small, high-quality, family-run school where values come first.

We also offer training programs for Kids and Youth that continue the same professional, community-focused path.

“Good character comes before knowledge.”

It matters to me that everyone who comes here knows there is a real person on the other side. I am available. I am not a system or artificial intelligence.

My name is Shabi Shilon, and this is the home I created here.

Talk to me, come meet us, and become part of my home.

— Shabi Shilon, founder and coach of Lucky Roll13

Coaching team

Bar Sarusi — Boxing and Muay Thai coach

Certified teacher Bachelor's degree in Psychology Boxing Muay Thai

Bar Sarusi is a certified teacher with a Bachelor's degree in Psychology, and at Lucky Roll13 he coaches the Youth Boxing group, the Women's Boxing group, and Muay Thai sessions. . Bar also provides personal Boxing training.

Although he is young, Bar began martial arts training early, attended training camps in Thailand, and gained practical Boxing experience.

Alongside his professional experience, Bar is a caring, patient, and dedicated teacher. His goal is to help every student improve their ability, technique, and self-confidence.
