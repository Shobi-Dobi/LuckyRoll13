A few words from me

This is the home I created here

I opened Lucky Roll13 for the community.

My goal is to reach as many hearts as possible. To be a teacher, bring people together, and create a place where members of my community are there for one another.

When I opened Lucky Roll13, I did not want to create another place where people learn only to fight. I wanted to create a school that develops the teachers of the future. The children growing up in my school today will be the teachers of tomorrow and beyond, community members who lead others and do good around them.

Lucky Roll13 is a school for Boxing and Jiu-Jitsu, and Mixed Martial Arts — MMA. It is a small, high-quality, family-run school where values come first.

I teach from ongoing experience in coaching, training, and competition in Israel and abroad. I have real experience, and I am fortunate to have been doing what I have always loved for more than 30 years.

“Good character comes before knowledge.”

It matters to me that everyone who comes here knows there is a real person on the other side. I am available. I am not a system or artificial intelligence.

My name is Shabi. I am a teacher and the founder of this Boxing, Jiu-Jitsu, and MMA school, and this is the home I created here.

Talk to me, come meet us, and become part of the home I created here.

— Shabi, teacher and founder

Coaching team

Bar Sarusi — Boxing and Muay Thai coach

Certified teacher Bachelor's degree in Psychology Boxing Muay Thai

Bar Sarusi is a certified teacher with a Bachelor's degree in Psychology, and at Lucky Roll13 he coaches the Youth Boxing group, the Women's Boxing group, and Muay Thai sessions. . Bar also provides personal Boxing training.

Although he is young, Bar began martial arts training early, attended training camps in Thailand, and gained practical Boxing experience.

Alongside his professional experience, Bar is a caring, patient, and dedicated teacher. His goal is to help every student improve their ability, technique, and self-confidence.
