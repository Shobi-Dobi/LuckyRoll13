MMA at Lucky Roll13

Integrated training that builds skill and confidence

Training is designed for gradual progress and a strong technical foundation, fitness, control, coordination, and confidence. It suits Kids and Youth who want to learn martial arts in a professional and supportive environment.

Technique

Discipline and confidence

MMA for Kids in Krayot

The groups suit families looking for MMA classes for kids in Kiryat Motzkin, Kiryat Bialik, Kiryat Yam, and across Krayot. You can start with no previous experience.

The first class introduces the training environment and safety rules, covers fundamentals at a gradual pace, and helps assess the right group. Send the child's age and whether they have previous experience before the visit so we can guide you accurately.

For more youth programs, read about Kids and Youth training.

Classes are held only in Kiryat Motzkin for ages 8–13: Tuesday 14:30–15:30 and Friday 12:00–13:00. For all other classes and locations, see the full training schedule.

Frequently asked questions

Kids MMA in Krayot — what should parents know?

Is previous experience required?

No. Kids and Youth can start with no previous experience and gradually learn stand-up and ground fundamentals.

What do kids learn in an MMA class?

Training combines Boxing, grappling, and Mixed Martial Arts fundamentals with movement, coordination, fitness, and discipline.

Where and when do Kids MMA classes take place?

MMA classes are held only in Kiryat Motzkin, at 26 Menachem Begin St., on Tuesdays 14:30–15:30 and Fridays 12:00–13:00.

How do I find the right group for my child?

Send the child's age and whether they have previous experience, and we will recommend the appropriate group and trial class.
