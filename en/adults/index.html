Training that progresses with you

Why personal training?

Personal training lets you focus on exactly what you need: fundamentals, technique, movement, fitness, correcting mistakes, and improving at your own pace.

Training suits complete beginners as well as experienced students who want greater precision and progress.

What do you get from a session?

Personal attention, training tailored to your level and goals, structured work, and gradual progress in Boxing or Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu.

For more about the disciplines, read about Boxing training and Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu training. You can also get to know the academy and coaching team and check the Kiryat Motzkin location details before booking a session.

Meet the coach

Bar Sarusi — Boxing and Muay Thai coach

Certified teacherBachelor's degree in PsychologyBoxingMuay Thai

Bar Sarusi is a certified teacher with a Bachelor's degree in Psychology, and coaches at Lucky Roll13 the Youth Boxing group, the Women's Boxing group, and Muay Thai sessions. In addition, Bar provides personal Boxing training for students at every level.

Despite his young age, Bar began martial arts early. Over the years he gained practical Boxing experience, attended training camps in Thailand, and continued developing his knowledge and skills as both a student and coach.

Beyond his professional experience, Bar stands out for his ability to teach. His background as a teacher, together with his patience, care, and personal attention, lets him tailor training to each student and build a gradual, professional, and safe process.

Want to improve your Boxing, get stronger, and build confidence? Join one of Bar’s groups or book a personal session with him, and get to work.
