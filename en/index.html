LUCKY ROLL13

Professional training. Personal attention. A real community.

Lucky Roll13 is a home for martial arts training for anyone who wants to learn and progress systematically, in a professional, personal, and supportive environment.

The academy was founded and is led by Shabi Shilon, a Boxing and Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (BJJ) coach who continues to train and compete in Israel and abroad.

You can join with no previous experience. Alongside beginners, we train kids, youth, and adults who want to develop, improve their skills, and make lasting progress.

Where do we train?

Lucky Roll13 serves students from across Krayot at two locations in Kiryat Motzkin.

Neve Ganim location in Kiryat Motzkin

The main location is at 26 Menachem Begin St. in Kiryat Motzkin's Neve Ganim neighborhood. It offers Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Boxing, MMA for kids, Women's Boxing, and personal training according to the published schedule.

Goshen Community Center location in Kiryat Motzkin

The new location is inside Goshen Community Center, 23A KKL St., Kiryat Motzkin. Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu classes are held on Mondays and Thursdays for children ages 5–7, children ages 8–13, and adults.

Frequently Asked Questions

Starting at Lucky Roll13

Do I need previous martial arts experience?

No. You can start with no previous experience. The class and group are selected according to age, level, and the discipline that interests you.

Which martial arts can I learn?

Our programs include Boxing, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (BJJ), Gi and No-Gi, MMA, Women's Boxing, and Kids and Youth programs.

Where do classes take place?

Classes are held at two locations in Kiryat Motzkin: 26 Menachem Begin St. and inside Goshen Community Center at 23A KKL St.

How do I start?

Send us a WhatsApp message with the discipline that interests you, your age and any previous experience, and we will recommend the right program.
