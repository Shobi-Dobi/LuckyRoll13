Frequently Asked Questions

Starting at Lucky Roll13

Do I need previous martial arts experience?

No. You can start with no previous experience. The class and group are selected according to age, level, and the discipline that interests you.

Which martial arts can I learn?

Our programs include Boxing, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (BJJ), Gi and No-Gi, MMA, Women's Boxing, and Kids and Youth programs.

Where do classes take place?

Our main Krayot program takes place at 26 Menachem Begin St., Kiryat Motzkin, with additional classes in Nesher.

How do I start?

Send us a WhatsApp message with the discipline that interests you, your age and any previous experience, and we will recommend the right program.
