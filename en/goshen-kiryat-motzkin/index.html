LUCKY ROLL13 · GOSHEN LOCATION

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu at Matnas Goshen, Kiryat Motzkin

The Lucky Roll13 location operates inside Matnas Goshen at 23A KKL St., Kiryat Motzkin. Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (BJJ) classes are held on Mondays and Thursdays in age-specific groups for children and adults.

A structured, community-focused Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu program for Kiryat Motzkin and the Krayot, with age-based groups and convenient fixed afternoon times.

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu groups at Goshen

Children ages 5–7

An age-appropriate Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu class on Mondays and Thursdays from 17:00 to 17:45 in a structured group for younger children.

Kids training information

Children ages 8–13

A Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu class for children and young teens on Mondays and Thursdays from 17:45 to 18:30, matched to this age range.

Kids and youth training information

Adults group

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu training for adults on Mondays and Thursdays from 18:30 to 19:30 in a professional group at a fixed time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently asked questions about the Goshen location

Where is the Lucky Roll13 Goshen location?

The location is at 23A KKL St., Kiryat Motzkin, inside Matnas Goshen.

Which days are classes held at Goshen?

Classes are held on Mondays and Thursdays.

What are the children’s Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu class times?

Children ages 5–7 train in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu from 17:00–17:45, and children ages 8–13 train from 17:45–18:30.

When is the adults Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu class?

The adults Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu class is held on Mondays and Thursdays from 18:30–19:30.

How do I book a trial class?

Send us a WhatsApp message or call 054-642-0206. We will recommend a group based on age and experience.
