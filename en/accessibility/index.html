Accessibility Statement

We believe it is important to make our website and services accessible to people with disabilities, and we work continuously to improve the experience.

Website accessibility

Website accessibility measures were implemented with reference to Israeli Standard 5568 and the WCAG guidelines, with the aim of meeting Level AA. The site was tested with automated tools and through keyboard, desktop, and mobile use.

Accessibility measures on the site

  • Semantic structure and ordered headings for navigation with assistive technologies.
  • A skip link that goes directly to the main content.
  • Keyboard navigation and operation of controls, including the navigation menu.
  • A clear visible indicator for the focused element.
  • Appropriate color contrast and resizable text.
  • Alternative text for meaningful images and labels for form fields.
  • A responsive display for different screen sizes and support for right-to-left text.
  • Reduced motion and animation according to the user’s device preference.

Third-party content

Some pages may include components from external sites, such as a YouTube player, maps, or payment services. Their accessibility also depends on the external provider. If you encounter difficulty, we will be happy to provide the information or service another way.

Accessibility at our training locations

Our training locations include Kiryat Motzkin and Nesher, and accessibility arrangements may differ by location. For current, accurate information before arriving about accessible parking, routes, entrances, and restrooms, contact us by phone or WhatsApp.

Contact us about accessibility

If you find an accessibility issue on the site or need information in an accessible format, please tell us which page caused difficulty and what adjustment you need. We will make every effort to respond promptly.
