Beginners and advanced students

A Women's Boxing class where beginners start right

Training combines technique, footwork, movement, fitness, and structured practice. You can join even if you have never boxed before.

The group is coached by Bar Sarusi, a certified teacher and Boxing and Muay Thai coach. Classes take place at Lucky Roll13, 26 Menachem Begin St., Kiryat Motzkin, and welcome women from Kiryat Bialik, Kiryat Yam, Kiryat Ata, and Kiryat Haim.

Regular class time: Friday, 09:00–10:00 in Kiryat Motzkin. Please book a trial class before arriving.

More information about technique and training options is available on our Boxing training page. You can also get to know the academy and coaching team, check the Kiryat Motzkin location details, and view the full training schedule before you visit.

Frequently asked questions

Women's Boxing in Krayot — before your first class

Are the classes suitable for beginners?

Yes. You can join with no previous experience, learn the fundamentals gradually, and progress at the right pace.

Who coaches the Women's Boxing group?

The Women's Boxing group at Lucky Roll13 is coached by Bar Sarusi, a certified teacher and Boxing and Muay Thai coach with a bachelor's degree in Psychology.

Where and when do the Women's Boxing classes take place?

Classes take place at Lucky Roll13, 26 Menachem Begin St., Kiryat Motzkin, on Fridays from 09:00 to 10:00.

What should I bring to a trial class?

Come in comfortable sportswear. Contact us before you arrive for precise equipment instructions and current group availability.
