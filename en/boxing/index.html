What do we learn?

The foundation of quality boxing

We build training around clear fundamentals that allow each student to progress systematically rather than simply working hard.

Technique

Build sound fundamentals, efficient movement, and accurate technique.

Footwork

Movement, balance, distance, and rhythm as part of boxing.

Accuracy and timing

Develop control, coordination, and the ability to work cleanly and under control.

Fitness

Improve endurance, work rate, and the ability to sustain effort throughout training.

For every level

Boxing for beginners and advanced students

Beginners

If this is your first time boxing, we will begin with the basics and build your skills gradually. You do not need a particular fitness level or previous background.

Experienced students

For those who have already trained, the goal is to identify what needs improvement and continue developing technique, movement, accuracy, and fitness.

Personal Training

For anyone who prefers more focused attention, there is also the option ofpersonal boxing training tailored to your goal and level.

Frequently Asked Questions

Before you start training

Can I start Boxing with no previous experience?

Yes. You can start from zero and progress gradually according to your level and personal pace.

Do you offer personal boxing training?

Yes. Alongside group classes, personal training is available for anyone who wants more focused work.

Where does training take place?

Our main Krayot program takes place at 26 Menachem Begin St., Kiryat Motzkin, and additional classes are available in Nesher.

How do I know which class is right for me?

Send us a short message with your age, any previous experience, and what you are looking for, and we will recommend the right program.
