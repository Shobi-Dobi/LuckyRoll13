Frequently Asked Questions

Martial arts for kids — questions parents ask

Considering martial arts classes for kids in Krayot? Here are answers to common questions about BJJ and MMA for kids at Lucky Roll13.

At what age can children start martial arts?

Children can start at a young age, depending on the group and their readiness. We adapt training to each student’s age, experience, and pace of progress.

Is previous BJJ or MMA experience required?

No. Children with no previous experience can join and gradually learn the fundamentals, technique, and training rules in a structured way.

Where do kids classes take place?

Kiryat Motzkin offers BJJ classes for ages 5–7 on Sundays and Wednesdays, 18:00–18:45; BJJ for ages 8–13 on the same days, 18:45–19:30; and MMA for ages 8–13 on Tuesday 14:30–15:30 and Friday 12:00–13:00. At the Goshen Community Center location classes are held on Mondays and Thursdays BJJ classes for ages 5–7 from 17:00–17:45 and ages 8–13 from 17:45–18:30.

What do children learn in training?

Alongside BJJ and MMA techniques, training emphasizes confidence, discipline, respect, coordination, fitness, perseverance, and working well with teammates.

Can we join a trial class?

Yes. Contact us to find a group suited to your child’s age and experience before getting started.

How do we register for kids martial arts?

Send us a WhatsApp message with your child’s age, preferred location, and any previous experience, and we will help you choose the right group.
